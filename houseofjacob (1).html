Who We Are

House of Jacob Ministries is a 508(c)(1)(A) Hebrew roots faith institution founded by Jacob Mason in New York. We are a non-denominational covenant community — not bound by the traditions of men, but anchored in the ancient Hebrew scriptures and the promises of the Most High.

We gather to worship, to study the Torah, to observe the appointed times, and to build a community that reflects the fullness of our sacred heritage. Our faith is not a religion we practice on Sunday mornings — it is a covenant we live every day.

Through House of Jacob, we also founded J.U.S. H.E.L.P.P. F.O.S.A.D — our criminal justice innovation division — because we believe that true faith is expressed in action. We serve our sons and daughters not just spiritually, but practically, legally, and economically.

  • One HouseWe are one family — diverse in background, unified in covenant. All who seek the ancient Hebrew roots are welcome here.
  • The Torah LivesWe study and honor the Torah, the prophets, and the writings as our living guide — not as historical documents but as covenant instruction for today.
  • The Appointed TimesWe observe the Feast Days and appointed times of the Most High, reconnecting with the ancient calendar of our ancestors.
  • Faith in ActionThrough community service, advocacy, and J.U.S. H.E.L.P.P. F.O.S.A.D, we put our covenant into practice in the world.
  • RestorationWe believe in the full restoration of our people — spiritually, economically, and communally. The Most High is faithful to His promises.

Our Ministry Leader

Jacob Mason

Founder & Ministry Leader

Jacob Mason is the founder of House of Jacob Ministries and the Executive Director of J.U.S. H.E.L.P.P. F.O.S.A.D. He carries a dual mandate — shepherding a covenant faith community and serving justice-involved sons and daughters in New York.

An IRS Enrolled Agent, software engineer, and life insurance professional, Jacob brings a rare combination of spiritual vision and institutional discipline to everything he builds. House of Jacob Ministries is his foundation — the covenant from which all else flows.
